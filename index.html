                  About Channel Flip and where the data comes from

                  Channel Flip is a free fan project and is not affiliated with MyAnimeList. Data © MyAnimeList and the dataset authors below. The catalog is every anime listed on MyAnimeList as of July 2026 (28,779 titles), from the open anime-offline-database, with explicit adult titles left out. English titles, scores and member counts come from MyAnimeList through the meesvandongen/anime-dataset snapshot of October 2026. A show keeps its Japanese title when MyAnimeList has no English one. Shows MyAnimeList hasn't scored yet use the average across MyAnimeList, AniList, Kitsu and other sites, marked "AVG SCORE". "First seasons only" is a best guess from titles and tags, so the odd sequel may slip through. "If you like this" picks come first from AniList's community recommendations (what viewers voted for), then from shows with the closest detailed tags (themes, setting, tone and more), leaving out other entries from the same series. "Describe a mood" matches your words against each show's detailed tags; mention a title ("like Frieren but darker") to start from that show. Synopses load live from the free Jikan MyAnimeList API, and cover images from MyAnimeList. Time-to-finish uses MyAnimeList's average episode length. Every pick links to its MyAnimeList page. Your watchlist, loved and seen marks stay in this browser.
