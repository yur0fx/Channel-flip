                  Channel Flip is a free fan project and is not affiliated with MyAnimeList. The manga list is every manga, manhwa, manhua and light novel on MyAnimeList, downloaded weekly through the free Jikan API, with explicit adult titles and doujinshi left out. Scores, member counts, authors and covers come from MyAnimeList. "Describe a mood" matches your words against each title's genres, themes and story keywords; mention a title ("dark manhwa like Solo Leveling") to start from it. Reading time assumes about 5 minutes per chapter. Your loved, watchlist and read marks stay in this browser and are separate from the anime ones.
